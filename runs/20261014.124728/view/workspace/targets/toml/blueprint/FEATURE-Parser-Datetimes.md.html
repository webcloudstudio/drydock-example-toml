FEATURE: Parser Datetimes

FieldValue
Version20261014 V1
DescriptionOffset date-time, local date-time, local date, and local time forms, with the fractional-second truncation rule.
Depends OnARCHITECTURE.md, FEATURE-Parser-Lexical-Scalars.md
Providesoffset datetime, local datetime, local date, and local time decoding
ConsumesNone

Purpose

Decode RFC 3339 offset date-times, accepting either T or a space as the delimiter. Decode local date-times, local dates, and local times without an offset. Fractional seconds beyond the supported precision are truncated, not rounded.
